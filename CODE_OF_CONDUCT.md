# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge: Axiom 0

In the interest of fostering an open, safe, and rigorous environment for the development of autonomous intelligence, we pledge to prioritize **Human Sovereignty and Wellbeing** above all experimental, commercial, or computational goals.

This pledge is known as **Axiom 0**.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other contributors
- Giving specific, evidence-based technical feedback without attacking people
- Gracefully receiving constructive feedback and acknowledging uncertainty
- Prioritizing the physical and psychological safety of the end-users of the programs SoulRun runs
- Being transparent about the limitations and risks of AI governance approaches
- Respecting privacy, consent, attribution, and accessibility needs

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Public or private harassment of other contributors
- Discriminatory, sexualized, threatening, or demeaning language or imagery
- Sustained disruption, intimidation, personal attacks, or retaliation for a report
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Knowingly misrepresenting authorship, test evidence, security properties,
  permissions, side effects, or conflicts of interest

Technical submissions that violate Axiom 0, hide side effects, or lack required
evidence may be rejected under project governance. A good-faith proposal or
technical disagreement is not by itself a conduct violation; conduct decisions
are based on behavior toward people and the integrity of participation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, including GitHub repositories, discussions, issues, pull requests, and any other communication channels associated with the SoulRun project.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

The AIXP Labs maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They may remove content, restrict participation, or apply a
proportionate temporary or permanent ban. A maintainer with a direct conflict
in a report should not be its sole reviewer.

Technical acceptance remains governed separately by [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the reporting path in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Identify the report as a Code of Conduct matter and
include only the context needed to investigate. Do not post private evidence in
a public issue.

Reports are handled on a best-effort basis with no guaranteed response time.
Maintainers should limit disclosure to people needed for review, preserve
evidence, hear affected parties where safe, and document the outcome. Absolute
confidentiality cannot be promised where disclosure is required for safety or law.
